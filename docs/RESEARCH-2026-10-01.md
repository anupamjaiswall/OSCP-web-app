## Facts locked into V34.38

- 23h45 attack window; separate 24-hour report upload window.
- 3 standalone systems worth 20 points each and one 3-machine AD set worth 40 points; 70/100 remains the passing threshold.
- A proof must be obtained in an interactive shell and displayed with `cat` or `type` from its original location.
- The screenshot for each `local.txt` / `proof.txt` must include the proof contents and target IP (`ip addr`, `ifconfig`, or `ipconfig`).
- The exact proof value must also be submitted in the Exam Control Panel before the exam ends; the panel does not validate whether the submitted value is correct.
- AI/LLM chatbots remain prohibited during both the exam and the reporting phase.
- Metasploit modules / Meterpreter remain restricted to one selected target; Metasploit cannot be used for pivoting across the AD set.
- Current guide states 24 machine reverts, with the limit resettable once during the exam.

## V34.38 product decision

The existing Bank the Points dialog already contained the official proof requirements, but it was passive text. V34.38 adds a temporary five-step closure checklist and quick Linux/Windows capture commands. The checklist intentionally resets when opened and never writes a “points banked” state, because only OffSec can determine whether evidence is valid.
